Platform Features

Account & Login

  1. Guest mode: tap "Play as Guest" + name. Scores saved on this device only.
  2. Google sign-in: profile pill → "Sign in with Google" → choose account. Name, avatar, and scores sync across devices.
  3. Sign out: profile → "Sign Out". Local data stays.
  4. Play without login: always works — login is 100% optional.

🎱 BallMerge

Puzzle30 levels

Goal

Drop numbered balls into columns. Two same-number balls touching merge into the next number. Build chains and reach ★ (level 7). Game over when any column fills to the top.

Controls

  • Click/tap a column to drop the next ball
  • Mouse-hover shows preview placement

Scoring

  • Each merge = level² × 120 × bonus
  • Higher levels = bigger bonus multiplier

Adventure

Hit the score target before any column overflows. 30 levels, fewer rows, more colors, higher targets.

Tips

  • Stack same-number balls in adjacent columns to chain merges
  • Don't waste a column on lone balls — merge potential matters

🎨 ChromaMatch

Match-330 levelsTimed

Goal

Swap adjacent tiles to match 3+ same color. Cleared tiles fall, new ones drop. Cascading combos = doubled scores!

Controls

  • Tap a tile to select (glows white)
  • Tap an adjacent tile to swap

Scoring

  • Match-3 = 100, Match-4 = 300, Match-5+ = 600
  • Each cascade depth × 2 multiplier

Adventure

Hit score target before timer runs out. 30 levels, bigger boards, more colors, less time.

🏗️ StackTower

Arcade30 levelsReflex

Goal

Tap to drop a sliding block onto your tower. Only the overlap stays. Land a perfect (⭐ PERFECT!) to keep full width.

Controls

  • Click/tap/Space to drop

Scoring

1 floor = 1 point. Adventure: hit target floor count.

Tips

  • Time the drop on the slowest part of the slide
  • Perfect stacks stay full-width — chase them!

🃏 MemoryFlip

Memory30 levelsTimed

Goal

Flip cards two at a time to find matching pairs. Match all pairs before time runs out.

Controls

  • Click/tap a card to flip it

Scoring

  • Each pair = 100 × combo + remaining time bonus
  • Consecutive matches build combo (×2, ×3, …)

Adventure

30 levels: 4×3 → 8×8 board. Higher levels = faster card flip-back.

🐍 SnakeRush

Arcade30 levelsTimed

Goal

Steer the snake. Eat 🍎 food to grow. Don't hit the walls or your tail. Beat the clock!

Controls

  • Arrow keys / WASD on desktop
  • Swipe on mobile (or use the on-screen D-pad)

Power-ups

  • ⚡ Slow — slows the snake briefly
  • ⭐ Bonus — instant +50 points
  • ✂️ Cut — halves your length (escape tight situations)

Adventure

30 levels: faster base speed, tighter time limit, higher score target.

🫧 BubbleBlast

Puzzle30 levels

Goal

Aim and shoot bubbles at the cluster. Match 3+ same-color to pop. Clear the board to advance.

Controls

  • Mouse move / drag — aim
  • Click / tap — fire

Specials (from level 3)

  • 💣 Bomb — pops every neighbor
  • 🌈 Rainbow — matches any color

Scoring

  • Match-3 = 30 × level · 5+ = ×2 · 8+ = ×3
  • Drops from ceiling = 5 × level each
  • Sub-level cleared = 2000 + 50 × shots remaining

FAQ

Where are scores stored? IndexedDB on device. Google sign-in adds cloud sync.

Can I play on mobile? Yes. Open the URL in any browser, or wrap the URL in a WebView for native apps.
