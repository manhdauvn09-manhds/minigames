Local Storage (IndexedDB)

Your browser automatically stores game data locally using IndexedDB technology:

  • ✅ Works offline — Play without internet connection
  • ✅ Fast access — Data loads instantly from your device
  • ✅ Private — Only accessible from your browser
  • ⚠️ Device-specific — Data doesn't sync to other devices (unless you sign in)
  • ⚠️ Clearing browser cache may delete local saves

Cloud Storage (When Signed In)

When you sign in with Google, your data is synced to our secure cloud servers:

  • ✅ Sync across devices — Play on phone, tablet, or desktop
  • ✅ Permanent backup — Scores saved indefinitely
  • ✅ Leaderboard tracking — Compete globally with verified scores
  • ✅ Account recovery — Restore your profile if needed

Clearing Local Data

To clear locally stored data:

  1. Open browser DevTools (F12 or Right-click → Inspect)
  2. Go to Application/Storage tab
  3. Find "IndexedDB" → minigames
  4. Right-click and select "Delete" to clear local storage
  5. Refresh the page
⚠️ Important: Clearing local storage removes game progress saved on this device only. Cloud scores (if signed in) are not affected.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Gameplay Questions

Can I play offline?

Yes! Games work offline if you've played them before (data cached locally). However, leaderboard submission requires internet connection.

Do I need to sign in to play?

No, signing in is completely optional. You can play all games as a guest, though your scores won't appear on global leaderboards.

What happens if I lose internet during gameplay?

Your local progress is saved. When reconnected, your score will sync to the cloud (if signed in).

Can I play the same level multiple times?

Yes! Replay any level anytime. Only your best score is recorded for leaderboards.

Account & Sign-In Questions

Is my email safe?

Yes! Your email is never shared publicly or used for marketing. Only your display name appears on leaderboards.

Can I sign in with other accounts?

Currently we support Google Sign-In only. Other methods may be added in the future.

How do I change my display name?

Your name comes from your Google account. Update it in Google Account Settings and it will reflect here automatically.

Can I delete my account?

Currently, account deletion must be done through your Google Account settings. Contact support for assistance.

Leaderboard & Scoring Questions

Why isn't my score on the leaderboard?

Scores are recorded for signed-in players only. If you're signed in, it may take a few seconds to appear (page refresh helps).

Can scores be lost or reset?

Once recorded, scores are permanent. They're only removed if detected as fraudulent or by special request.

How often do leaderboards update?

Leaderboards update in real-time as new scores are submitted. Weekly rankings reset every Monday.

Technical Questions

What browsers are supported?

Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge (latest versions recommended). Mobile browsers fully supported.

Why is the game running slow?

Check: (1) Internet connection, (2) Browser performance (close other tabs), (3) Device storage (clear cache if full).

Can I access games on mobile?

Absolutely! All games are fully responsive and optimized for phones and tablets.

Do games have ads?

No intrusive ads! The platform is completely ad-free for a clean gaming experience.
